Global Partnerships

We actively cultivate Global Partnerships to enhance educational opportunities for our students. Through collaborations with esteemed institutions worldwide. We facilitate access to diverse academic resources and programs. These partnerships provide students with unique opportunities for cross-cultural exchanges, joint research initiatives, and collaborative projects.

Master's Desk

We offer exciting opportunity for students facilitating the pursuit of advanced degrees abroad at prestigious universities. This initiative enables students to immerse themselves in different cultures, gain valuable international experience, and enhance their academic and professional prospects. DSU encourages students to engage with international peers and develop a global perspective essential for success in today’s interconnected world.

Student Exchange

We provide a fantastic opportunity for students through its Student Exchange program, allowing them to study a semester abroad at partner universities worldwide. This initiative enables students to immerse themselves in new cultures, gain international experience, and broaden their academic horizons.

International Immersion

This program allows students to engage in cross-cultural experiences. This initiative encourages students to immerse themselves in diverse environments, gaining valuable global perspectives and hands-on experience in their fields of study. By participating in international immersion, DSU students can enhance their academic knowledge, build essential skills, and foster connections with peers from around the world.

Dayananda Sagar Institutions was founded in the 1960s, and Dayananda Sagar University (DSU) was established in 2014. DSU is renowned for its world-class interdisciplinary programs, including Medicine, Engineering, Business, Design, Computing, Law, and Science. It has also been accredited with a NAAC A+ rating.
