Welcome to the Future of Entrepreneurship!

The International Startup Entry Community (ISEC) at Dayananda Sagar University (DSU) is an exciting new initiative designed to empower student entrepreneurs and fuel innovation on a global scale. This proposal outlines the vision and strategy behind the creation of a platform that connects DSU students with international mentors, investors, and entrepreneurial opportunities.

Our Vision & Objectives

To transform DSU into a globally recognized hub for scalable innovation, where students can connect, create, and lead successful international ventures.

  • Foster a Global Entrepreneurial Mindset:
    Encouraging students to think big and innovate beyond local boundaries.
  • Connect Students with Mentors & Investors:
    Providing access to global experts and capital to turn ideas into reality.
  • Entrepreneurs with the Tools for Success:
    Offering the resources, guidance, and funding needed to build sustainable startups.
  • Expand to Global Markets:
    Helping DSU startups make an impact across international borders.
Why DSU?

Dayananda Sagar University is known for its innovation-driven culture, with cutting-edge resources and a passionate student body. With access to advanced incubation centers, academic excellence, and a leadership committed to fostering entrepreneurship, DSU is the ideal launchpad for ISEC.
Our vibrant community is already brimming with potential, and now it's time to bring those ideas into the global spotlight!

The Need for ISEC

Every year, countless student startups face the struggle of funding, scaling, and gaining visibility. Many brilliant ideas never reach their potential because of lack of access to mentors, investors, and global exposure. This is where ISEC steps in—offering students the necessary guidance, connections, and financial backing to take their ventures to new heights.

Inspiration from Global Successes

Global initiatives like the Ivy Growth Association have proven that the right mentorship and investor support can help regional ecosystems flourish. Inspired by such success stories, ISEC will focus on empowering students from all backgrounds, including those from metropolitan and Tier-2/Tier-3 cities, to build global startups.

What Sets Us Apart?
  1. Global Exposure
    Connecting DSU startups to international markets, allowing them to scale beyond India.
  2. World-Class Mentorship
    Building a strong network of mentors—including DSU alumni and global industry experts—who will provide guidance and strategic advice.
  3. Investment Readiness
    Helping students understand valuation, pitching, and fundraising to prepare them for successful investment rounds.
  4. Decentralized Discovery
    Establishing startup cells within departments, fostering innovation at the classroom level.
  5. Structured Support
    Offering access to workspace, legal/financial guidance, and micro-funding for student startups to accelerate their growth.
  6. Community Culture
    Creating a collaborative environment where ideas thrive, students learn from each other, and innovation becomes a way of life.
