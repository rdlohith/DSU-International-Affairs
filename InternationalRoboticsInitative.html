International Robotics Initiative

International Robotics Initiative guided by Dr. Sandeep Patil from RWTH University.

Come to Dayananda Sagar University to learn, create and innovate with the very best. Leave with the passion, connections, credentials and lifelong friends who will help you change the world.

Robotics is a rapidly growing field that offers immense potential for innovation, especially in addressing real-world challenges. This proposal outlines the plan to start a robotics programme at DSU, guided by Dr. Sandeep Patil from RWTH University. The programme will foster interdisciplinary collaboration and focus on solving pressing issues globally, such as water scarcity, air pollution, and traffic congestion, using advanced technologies.

The Robotics Initiative will be open to students from all engineering streams who are keen on working with robotics and new technologies. Monthly events will be conducted to encourage students to form teams and develop robotic solutions. The Initiative will focus on real-world applications of robotics, with the objective of addressing challenges globally.

The Office of International Relations will help you apply for the International Robotics Initiative. Click here to apply now!

Bootcamp For Innovators

This program features startup founders and industry experts who will share valuable insights into the latest developments in the global startup ecosystem.

Dayananda Sagar Institutions was founded in the 1960s, and Dayananda Sagar University (DSU) was established in 2014. DSU is renowned for its world-class interdisciplinary programs, including Medicine, Engineering, Business, Design, Computing, Law, and Science. It has also been accredited with a NAAC A+ rating.
